## Long description

**H2: Safety Standards & Protection Level**

- Certification name (e.g. EN 1621-2) and a short explanation
- Protection level, what it means in practice, and a brief comparison with the other levels
- **Limitation notice:** protects the back only, not the ribs or chest

**H2: Riding Use Cases**

- Use cases as described by the brand (e.g. hacking, schooling, showjumping, dressage)
- **Not** for cross-country and not a replacement for a body protector, unless the brand states otherwise
- End with the fixed governing body sentence

**H2: Sizing & Fit**

- Sizes (clothing size or age) and back length
- Size chart table, *only if the brand provides one*. Figures only; no measuring instructions.
- Adjustment points (belt, straps)
- One short closing sentence pointing to free in-store fitting and the advice line 01429 864112 (no links)

**H2: Freedom of Movement**

- Thickness and flexibility of the protective panel; movement while riding

**H2: Weight & Profile**

- Weight, *only if the brand states it*; whether it sits neatly under a show jacket

**H2: Breathability**

- Gilet fabric and breathability, *if any*

**H2: Ease of Use**

- Fastening (zip, belt); whether it can be put on alone

**H2: Other Highlights** *(only if there are 2 or more valid other features, following the Other features rule)*

- One paragraph per feature, in priority order

**H2: Colours**

- Same rules as Outline A

**H2: Durability, Care & Replacement**

- Lifespan; washing (is the outer machine washable, is the foam removable); storage; when to replace
