## Long description

**H2: Safety Standards & Protection Level**

- Certification name (e.g. BETA 2018, EN 13158:2018) and a short explanation of what it covers
- Protection level, what it means in practice, and a brief comparison with the other levels
- Areas of the body protected
- Check the label year carefully: 2009 or 2018. If the sources conflict, report it as a conflict.

**H2: Riding Use Cases**

- Use cases as described by the brand
- Whether it can be used in competition: *BETA 2018 Level 3 only*
- Always end with this fixed sentence: "Always check the current rules of your governing body before competing."

**H2: Air Vest Compatibility** *(only if the brand confirms it)*

- Which air vest it works with, and how it attaches

**H2: Sizing & Fit**

- Available sizes and lengths (front, back); women's or tall versions, *if any*
- Size chart in a table, *only if the brand provides one*. Show the figures only; do not add measuring instructions.
- Adjustment points
- How the protector should sit on the body and how close it should fit, *only if the brand states it for this product*
- One short closing sentence pointing to sizing help: Body Protector Finder (*only if File (2) confirms the product is included*), free in-store fitting and the advice line 01429 864112 (no links in this H2)

**H2: Freedom of Movement**

- Panel construction (segmented, hexagonal, articulated) and flexibility
- Movement across the shoulders, back and waist; in the riding position and when jumping

**H2: Weight & Profile**

- Weight or comparison, *only if the brand states it*
- How slim it is; whether it can be worn under a show jacket

**H2: Breathability**

- Mesh, lining, ventilation zones; heat management on longer rides

**H2: Ease of Use**

- Fastening type; whether it can be put on and taken off alone
- Correct use, *if the brand gives instructions*

**H2: Other Highlights** *(only if there are 2 or more valid other features, following the Other features rule)*

- One paragraph per feature, in priority order

**H2: Colours**

- List every colour available on the site, using the exact colour names from the Colours column in File (2). Do not rename or translate them.
- If only one colour is available, state it in one sentence.
- Style notes (e.g. a colour chosen to match competition jackets), *only if the brand states them*
- Do not describe fabric, construction or features here.

**H2: Durability, Care & Replacement**

- Lifespan as recommended by the brand
- Washing, drying and storage
- What to check after a fall, when to replace, spare parts *if any*
